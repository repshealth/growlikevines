It's not just what you do.

It's how you do it, when you do it and why you do it. Nutrition, training, sleep and your daily routine aren't separate pieces of the puzzle. They influence each other. Project Reset brings them together into one approach, working towards the same outcome.

Neurological

Change how your brain thinks, learns, responds and drives your behaviour.

Physiological

Change how your body functions, responds, recovers and adapts.

Nervous system

Improve your ability to handle stress, return to calm and recover properly.

One day.
Three shifts.

Every day follows the same rhythm. The Morning and Evening set it, the Day runs on your food and training. You open the app, follow the next step and tick it off. Every tick logs the time for you.

↑Morning

Switch on, bring it back down, open the day.

  • Your action, planned the night before: cold therapy, sauna, cardio, a workout or breath work
  • Your low arousal task to bring it back down
  • Meal 1 within 90 minutes of waking
→Day

Eat, move, train. Stay centred.

  • Each meal built the Project Reset way, then a walk block straight after
  • Your training session, with steps before, during and after
  • Close the day and set up tomorrow
↓Evening

Wind down. Recover for tomorrow.

  • Your evening meal
  • Low arousal tasks, lights dimmed, phone away
  • Rate where your nervous system is, aiming for calm

What you get

Everything lives in one app on your phone. Simply stick to the rules set for each day, and complete as much as you can. Every completed task earns points.

The daily process board

Morning, Day and Evening, one step at a time. You're always shown what's next, with an info button on every task explaining why it matters. Skip a step if you need to: you'll be prompted, never blocked.

Targets set by Ellis

Your daily steps, protein and water, set by Ellis from your onboarding form. Track them as you go and see what's left.

Meal plans and a shopping list

Build a training day and a rest day plan from the approved foods, or start from Ellis's example. The app checks every meal against the rules and builds your shopping list.

Training with structure

Set-up steps before you train, cues during your session and a close-out after it, so every workout is driven with intent.

Learn to shift your state

Rate your nervous system from calm to wired, and notice the senses you've used. Over 21 days you learn what switches you on and what brings you down.

Your score and check-ins

A daily score for everything you complete, your progress across all 21 days, plus check-ins on Day 7, 14 and 21 so you and Ellis can see what's changing.

Questions

What happens after I pay?

You go straight to your onboarding form. Once you've sent it, Ellis reviews your answers and sets your daily targets. When they're ready, you log in, watch 3 short videos and plan your first day that evening.

Is it an app I download?

It runs in your phone's browser, so there's nothing to install. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like any other app.

Do I have to follow a set diet?

No. You build your own meals from the Project Reset food lists, following a few simple rules. You can save a training day and a rest day plan, or start from Ellis's example, and the app fills your meals in for you.

What if I miss a step or have an off day?

Nothing locks you out. The app prompts you through each step, and anything you skip just lowers that day's score. The more you complete, the higher it goes.

Do I need a gym?

You train with your own workout for now, and the app gives you the structure around it: set-up steps before, cues during and a close-out after.

Who sees my answers and photos?

Only Ellis and the coaching team. Photos are optional.
