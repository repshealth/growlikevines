What

What you're doing. Every task in your day, from your morning action to your evening meal.

How

When

The rules behind it

Simply stick to the rules set for each day. The app checks what it can for you, and prompts you through the rest.

Food

Energy in, energy out, recover, repeat. No movement, no food.

  • Carbs pair with training, fats pair with recovery. Mixing them up in the wrong place disrupts your nervous system.
  • Carbs only before and after training, and in your evening meal. Pre-workout carbs fuel the session, post-workout carbs restore your glycogen, and carbs before bed are always fine.
  • Never direct carbs and fats in the same meal.
  • Fruit and veg at least 3 times a day, with no maximum.
  • Calm before you eat, move after. Have carbs and sit at your desk, then have carbs and go for a walk, and notice the difference.

Training

All effort driven by glycogen-fuelled muscle. No energy wasted on your nervous system through poor muscle connection or going beyond failure.

  • Set up: clear and set your mind, write the workout down, then change your clothes, music and environment.
  • Start: movement prep for balance and body awareness, then a powerful, max effort first movement to signal demand to the muscle.
  • During: blood flow and constant tension, breath directing every rep, and visualising the muscle you're working.
  • Close: release tension, a slow walk breathing deep into your stomach, lower your body temperature and change your environment.
